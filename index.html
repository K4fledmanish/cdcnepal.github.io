Dedicated to helping the helpless in Nepal

Serving Kathmandu since 2011

Community Development Center – CDC Nepal exists to bring comfort, dignity, and hope to the most vulnerable members of Nepali society.

We support elderly people residing in care homes across the Kathmandu Valley who lack basic necessities like food, warm clothing, and proper healthcare, as well as helpless children who need access to formal education and loving guidance.

Safe & transparent funding

To ensure 100% financial transparency and security for all international donors, CDC Nepal processes all public funding exclusively through GlobalGiving (Org ID: #13550). We've been vetted since 2012 and currently hold Top Ranked status, GlobalGiving's recognition for consistent engagement and program effectiveness.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask before donating or getting involved with CDC Nepal.

Is CDC Nepal a verified non-profit organization?
Yes. CDC Nepal has been a vetted non-profit partner on GlobalGiving, a leading global crowdfunding platform for nonprofits, since 2012 (Org #13550), and currently holds GlobalGiving's Top Ranked status for consistent engagement and program effectiveness. All donations are processed securely through GlobalGiving.
How is my donation used?
Donations fund three ongoing programs: food, clothing, and medical care for elderly residents in Kathmandu care homes; tuition, uniforms, and textbooks for helpless and orphaned children; and emergency relief for families affected by flooding in Nepal.
How do I know my donation is secure?
All public funding is processed exclusively through GlobalGiving, an independent, vetted platform, rather than directly by CDC Nepal. This keeps every transaction transparent and auditable.
Can I sponsor a specific child or elderly resident?
Contributions go toward program-wide funds rather than individual sponsorships, so support reaches whichever children or elderly residents currently need it most. Reach out via the contact details below if you'd like to discuss a dedicated gift.
Where is CDC Nepal based?
CDC Nepal is based in Kathmandu Metropolitan City, Bagmati Province, Nepal, and has been operating since 2011.
